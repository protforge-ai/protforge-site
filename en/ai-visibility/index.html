Sources

Which pages the engines cite when they talk about your category: directories, comparison sites, forums, press. That is where you need to be, and it is often not your own site.

FAQ

How is this different from regular SEO?

Classic SEO fights for a position in a list of links. Here the result is a written answer that either names you or doesn't. It rests on the same foundations (clear content, a crawlable site, good sources), but it is measured differently and rewards other things: structured data, pages that answer specific questions, and mentions on the sources the engines trust. That is why we work both layers at once.

How long until I see results?

Technical fixes (crawler access, structured data, content readable without JavaScript) can show up in weeks. Mentions and citations tied to new content or third-party sources usually take one to three months. Monthly tracking re-runs the same questions, so you can see what each change actually moved.

Which engines do you measure?

ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews, with the same question set on each. If your buyers use another one (Gemini, Copilot), we add it.

Do you guarantee I'll be recommended?

No, and be wary of anyone who does. These engines are not deterministic: the same question can return different answers two days apart. What we do is measure on a large sample, remove what is blocking you, and improve the signals you can control. You get the before and after numbers, good or bad.

What do you need from me to start?

Your website, two or three competitors, and a sentence on who you sell to and where. We write the questions and show them to you before running them. We don't need access to your analytics or your CMS for the audit.

Do you work white-label for agencies?

Yes. SEO and marketing agencies use us for the measurement and technical side, with a report under their brand and no visibility to the end client. Write to us and we'll set it up.
