Everything we do starts from the same question: can every output be traced back to something true? We build the measurement first, then fix what it finds.

About ProtForge

ProtForge started in 2023 as a generative-AI company for therapeutic antibody design: diffusion models, graph neural networks and protein language models on AWS. The science taught us one habit we never dropped: a prediction nobody can verify is worth nothing.

Today ProtForge is an independent engineering and advisory practice led by Guillermo Llopis, a two-time startup CTO and PhD researcher in LLM hallucination detection. He has shipped production multi-agent LLM platforms where every figure in every output traces back to a source document, and he brings that same evaluation discipline to every client system.

We build on Vercel, Supabase, Cloudflare Workers, AWS and the Anthropic and OpenAI APIs, and we are vendor-neutral when it comes to which model you should actually run.
